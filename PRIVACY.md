# Privacy Policy

All audio is processed on-device. Audio buffers are never saved or transmitted.

## Live Link

When enabled, captions are streamed end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) via a local server and optional SSH tunnel. The encryption key is in the URL fragment and never sent to the server.

## Local Storage

Preferences and optional autosaved transcripts are stored locally. No analytics or telemetry.
